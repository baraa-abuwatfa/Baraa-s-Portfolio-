🌟 About the Project

AI CFO is an AI-powered financial management platform designed to help small businesses, entrepreneurs, and company owners manage their financial activities and gain a clearer understanding of their business performance.

Traditional financial management systems often focus on recording and displaying financial transactions. AI CFO takes this further by combining financial management with intelligent analysis and AI-assisted decision support.

The platform enables users to:

- Manage company income and expenses.
- Monitor financial performance through interactive dashboards.
- Analyze revenue, expenses, and profitability.
- Identify potentially unusual expenses.
- Generate monthly and annual financial reports.
- Ask financial questions using natural language.
- Receive explanations and recommendations based on available financial data.

The main idea behind AI CFO is simple:

Financial data should not only be recorded. It should be understood and transformed into meaningful decisions.

❗ The Problem

Small businesses and startups frequently face financial management challenges, especially when they lack dedicated financial analysis resources.

Some of the most common challenges include:

Challenge| Business Impact
Manual financial tracking| Consumes time and effort
Limited financial visibility| Makes business performance difficult to understand
Difficulty analyzing expenses| Makes unnecessary spending harder to identify
Lack of financial comparisons| Limits understanding of financial trends
Delayed financial reporting| Slows down decision-making
Complex financial information| Makes financial insights less accessible

Business owners need more than a system that stores financial records. They need a tool that helps them understand what is happening financially and why it matters.

💡 Our Solution

AI CFO provides an integrated financial management environment that connects financial records with intelligent analysis.

Instead of reviewing transactions individually and calculating financial indicators manually, users can access summarized financial information, explore trends, identify potential issues, and interact with an AI Financial Assistant.

The solution focuses on three main areas:

Analysis Capabilities

Financial Alerts| Bring attention to important financial changes
AI Recommendations| Provide analysis-based suggestions

Unusual Expense Detection

The platform evaluates expense records to identify potentially unusual spending.

The configured detection approach considers an expense unusual when it is at least twice the usual category average, provided there are at least three previous expenses in that category.

This helps identify expenses that may deserve further review.

Financial Alerts

The analysis can highlight conditions such as:

- Expenses exceeding revenue.
- Expenses increasing by 20% or more.
- Revenue decreasing by 20% or more.
- Potentially unusual expenses.

These alerts are intended to help users notice financial changes that may require attention.

«Alerts indicate potential financial concerns and should be reviewed in their business context.»

Step-by-Step Process

1. The user registers or logs into the platform.
2. The user creates or accesses a company.
3. The system determines the user's company membership and permissions.

6. Financial Intelligence analyzes financial performance and highlights potential issues.

8. Users review the results and use them to support business decisions.
