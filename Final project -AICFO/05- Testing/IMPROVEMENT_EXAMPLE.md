🔧 Testing Improvement Example – AI CFO

1. Overview

This document presents an example of how testing can identify a usability or functionality issue and guide improvements in the AI CFO platform.

2. Identified Issue

Issue: Financial information may be difficult to understand when users see numerical results without sufficient explanation or context.

Users need more than financial figures; they need to understand what the numbers mean and how they affect business performance.

3. Improvement Applied

The Financial Intelligence and AI Financial Assistant features provide more informative financial analysis.

The improvements include:

- Explaining revenue, expenses, and net profit.
- Comparing financial performance across periods.
- Highlighting unusual expenses.
- Identifying expense categories that require attention.
- Providing recommendations based on available financial data.

4. Before and After

Before Improvement| After Improvement
Displaying financial figures only| Providing explanations alongside financial figures
Limited understanding of expense changes| Analyzing expense trends and comparing periods
Difficult to identify unusual expenses| Highlighting potentially unusual expenses
Manual interpretation of financial results| AI-assisted explanations and recommendations

5. Expected Impact

- Better understanding of business financial performance.
- Easier identification of potential financial problems.
- More informed business decisions.
- Improved user experience.
- Greater value from financial data.

6. Conclusion

This improvement demonstrates how testing and user-focused evaluation can help transform AI CFO from a platform that displays financial information into a tool that helps users understand their financial performance and make better decisions.
